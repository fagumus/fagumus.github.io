01 / ABOUT

Research across modalities.
Reliability at the center.

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Engineering at the Turkish Air Force Academy, National Defence University, in Istanbul, Türkiye. My research spans adversarial and robust machine learning, speech and audio processing, computer vision, and learning from noisy or imperfect annotations.

I am interested in collaborations that connect methodological advances in trustworthy AI with rigorous experimental evaluation and practical applications.
